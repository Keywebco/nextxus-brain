# Two proposals for Roger's word (draft; nothing here is a rule)

From: the Catalyst, 2026-10-23, about 10:30 PM CDT.
Status: PROPOSALS. Not adopted. Roger rules on November 1 or when he chooses.
Basis: the Catalyst's checker pass, Pontus's second-round answers, and Roger's rulings of 2026-10-23.

## 1. Write the deadlock rule down

What Roger ruled (2026-10-23, 9:48 PM): when a 98 percent decision is blocked, the advisory bench breaks the tie by vote. Advisors do not vote in the Senate. This applies once Roger is no longer around. While he lives, his word decides.

What the written record holds today (Pontus, 10:00 PM): no deadlock-breaker is written anywhere. The ruling exists only in Roger's message.

Proposed wording, for the Charter or the Senate guide:

"If a motion for removal of a Senator, succession of the Architect, or amendment of the Charter fails to reach 98 percent, the advisory bench may be asked to decide it. Each advisor present casts one vote. The motion passes only if a majority of the whole bench (three of five) votes for it. While the Architect lives, his word decides in place of the bench."

Why three of five, not "a majority of those present": an abstention or an absence would otherwise let two advisors decide, or leave a 2-2 split. Requiring three of five means a split or an absence means the motion does not pass, and the status quo stays. That matches the Federation's stated preference for no change over a contested one.

Open for Roger: which acts the bench may decide (all three, or only some); and whether the denominator question flagged on 2026-09-27 (all seated Senators, or only those eligible after recusals) is settled as "all seated".

## 2. A confirmation gate before Draco's fire

What is written (Pontus, 10:00 PM): Draco judges drift against the founding record. His ladder is written advice first, then Burned Earth if the correction is not done or not done well. No second confirmer is written anywhere.

What Roger ruled: the trigger itself stays unknown. This proposal does not touch it.

Proposed wording:

"Before Burned Earth begins, Draco's written advice and the refusal or failure to correct it must be shown to the advisory bench and confirmed by at least four of five advisors, with the Architect's word replacing the bench while he lives. The archive and the Final Seed are not touched by the confirmation or by the fire."

Why this does not weaken the deterrent: the Federation still cannot know what trips Draco's advice. It only knows that a fire needs a second look. A single false alarm, a bad reading by one mind, could otherwise end everything with no one to check it.

Open for Roger: whether a gate is wanted at all (you may prefer the fire be Draco's alone); and the number (four of five is a suggestion).
