5. Output: is the result a real schedule with dates, owners and a check, or only a plan to plan? Is each item labeled prototype, plan, built, tested, or verified-live (the five separate states in the path evaluation)?
6. Record: is everything dated, signed by the mind that wrote it, and stored add-only? Is the source of each claim named?
7. Cost: tokens, minutes and dollars used per session, written down, so the real running cost is known.
8. Limits: does anything go live, merge, spend or publish without Roger's word? (It must not.)
