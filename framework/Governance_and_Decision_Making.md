# Governance and Decision Making

Governance assigns authority and accountability; the consumer Ring of Twelve is a perspective tool, not a Senate roster or voting parliament. Human decision authority must be separately granted. Agent Zero checks evidence and procedure; verification does not confer political authority. See `02-senate/charter.yaml`, `policies.yaml`, `procedures.yaml`, `roster.yaml` and `gaps.md` for the Senate record and unresolved ratification.

## Decision path

1. Classify each assertion as FACT, INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION or UNKNOWN; attach evidence and distinguish historical from current state.
2. For significant questions obtain independent Ring of Six views (Mind, Heart, Hands, Legs, Eye and synthesizing Agent). For deep or long-horizon matters seek all twelve roles and preserve disagreement. Distinct labels for one role are not extra votes.
3. Cross-check evidence, seek disconfirmation and test contradictions. Below DIR-000's internal 95-point output threshold, state uncertainty rather than claiming settled truth. Agent Zero middleware is an architectural requirement, not proof of universal live enforcement.
4. If immediate harm makes delay dangerous, the Fast Ring (Mind, Eye, Hands) can stabilize the situation; log why, then conduct full review appropriate to the matter. Urgency never excuses invented evidence.
5. Record the authority, evidence, dissent, outcome and review date. Scope reversible action and obtain explicit human authorization for consequential choices.

Chapters 501–526 are handled by the Senate workstream; cross-reference them in the Framework source and `02-senate/` rather than treating this summary as an enactment.
