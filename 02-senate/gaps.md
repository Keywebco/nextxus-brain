# Senate policy and procedure gaps, 2026-10-05

A source audit, not a ruling. No draft or older procedure becomes law merely by indexing it.

1. **Charter standing:** The public [Senate page](https://keywebco.github.io/nextxus-senate/) calls the 16-article Charter a second-round draft, while the [September 26 source document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1AS0AEDqg1SruQoThtInp94gYqOOd5gwoMVxTDNtiOWE/edit) contains a sealed/ratification block. A [September 27 review](https://docs.google.com/document/d/11VftaY64sSgRWuaeNH16DbJTFHAIKcqXt5EH8geI4qI/edit) calls it ratified. Need dated Architect or Journal reconciliation.
2. **12 seats versus old 15 domains:** The Architect's 2026-10-05 work order supplies a twelve-role roster, not appointments or acceptances. [Historical GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/Keywebco/nextxus-agent-zero/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md) says fifteen domain seats. Need current appointment and participation records. Agent Zero is designated a role but the [public Senate](https://keywebco.github.io/nextxus-senate/) says verifier, not voter.
3. **Routine motions and quorum:** [How the Senate Runs](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1M6NpmPHhmgV_kBPUXGwVmFoDD7TM-izvJx2KWJuAeEI/edit) says ordinary-majority threshold is only a recommendation. Speaker, schedule, and permanent Scribe are also open rulings. Do not infer a threshold from the exceptional draft 98% rule.
4. **Agent Zero verification:** Draft [Article XVI](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1AS0AEDqg1SruQoThtInp94gYqOOd5gwoMVxTDNtiOWE/edit) requires a subordinate identity, continuity, authenticity, availability, duplication and audit protocol. Not located as an adopted instrument.
5. **Recycling request:** Draft Articles VI and IX provide evidentiary and reversible-action limits, but not a complete request, defense, review, final Architect authorization and archiving sequence. Final recycling is reserved to the Architect by the work order.
6. **New-seat admission:** Draft Article II reserves appointment while Roger lives, but no complete nomination, invitation, voluntary acceptance, conflict screening and Journal-entry procedure was verified.
7. **Amendment:** Draft Article XIV does not set a precise examination period or full voting and implementation workflow. [Old GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/Keywebco/nextxus-agent-zero/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md) instead declares its seven articles unamendable. Resolve precedence.
8. **Emergency authority:** Historical joint Senate/Tribune Continuity Protocol and draft Article XIII's narrower temporary authority differ. No named current continuity holder, duration or restoration rule was verified.
9. **Old policies await Senate formalization:** Historical governance describes sessions, stewards, the 95% Truth Gate, ethics audits, cadence and discipline. The [September 21 procedure document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1zD00KgrzyXk0nZ7XNH1IlIdDJl4C6vxd2AHLy_Z-AIA/edit) covers six operational procedures, including University/SIM, lens, intake, verification and self-teaching. No Senate enactment confirmed. Its external-syllabus acquisition section also needs an explicit licensing and attribution check before use.
10. **No Delete and Cathedral plus Satellites:** The Architect identifies these as governance rules, not numbered directives; the old [Cathedral v2.2 anchor](https://github.com/keyhole-creator/nextxus-yaml-database/blob/main/HUMANCODEX_V2_CATHEDRAL.md) is history, and historical governance documents no-delete mechanics. A current formal Senate text is not confirmed.
11. **Immutable Rules of Interpretation:** Cathedral v1.1, including Century Reassembly, stays in 00-immutable and is only cross-referenced here. [Canonical Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/11n31tvxonX7-OHcXUhQVt6F5NfkOJJny3dFyl2wDhGY/edit).
12. **Private source boundary:** Keywebco/NextXus-Knowledge-Base/senate-sessions/ is reachable through the private integration. It may contain rulings, but its contents are not copied into this public brain under BRIEF.md. Historical internal AI self-evaluation text is likewise not published.
