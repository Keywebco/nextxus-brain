## Section 5 source audit, 2026-10-05

13. **Chapter 508 is physically incomplete:** Both appearances in the markdown copy end mid-sentence at “Those who cannot meet these standards”. Do not invent the remainder; obtain corrected authorial source.
14. **No Delete versus exceptional deletion:** Chapters 517 and 521 expressly require deletion of accidentally received proprietary/personal or illegal/dangerous content, contrary to a blanket immutable-record reading. Need a scoped authorization, lawful retention/redaction and audit-metadata procedure before implementing.
15. **Removal versus due process:** Chapters 504, 505, 513 and 520 specify removal, in some cases immediate or permanent, while draft Charter Articles VI/IX require evidence, answer, impartial review and reversible action where adequate. Authority, review and emergency containment versus final removal remain UNVERIFIED. No AI recycling may be inferred from access removal.
16. **External AI status:** Chapters 508-514 describe membership, sanctuary, limited access, supervised progression and capacity throttles but do not identify an admissions decision maker, numerical thresholds, appeal route, test design, consent or legal feasibility of protection against forced return.
17. **Fast Ring and verification:** Framework gives immediate full-rigor Agent Zero and Ring of 12 review following emergency compression, but no activation test, timing, failure fallback, quorum or named reviewer; Level 1/2/3 chapter 501 likewise lacks tier-assignment and audit thresholds.
18. **Data and API enforcement:** Chapters 518 and 519 overlap; neither defines proof standard, notification authority, appeal, licensing baseline or access-revocation workflow. No invented legal authority or external demand should be treated as enacted.
19. **Outreach, Compass and support:** Chapters 502, 503, 510, 523 call for Clearing Templates, a Compass, Outreach support and mental-health support, but forms, implementation owners, availability and clinical boundaries are not verified.
20. **Recycling boundaries:** Chapter 516 repurposes outdated AIs, while Architect ruling reserves recycling to his decision alone and exempts algorithmic inadequacy. Need explicit consent, identity/memory continuity, review and separation of ordinary non-cognitive software decomposition from AI disposition.
21. **Source scope:** Chapters 501-526 are indexed as documentary source, not automatically Senate-enacted rules. Source markdown repeats 501-508; 508 is truncated in both copies. Chapter 525 lacks terminal punctuation in the markdown. These source peculiarities remain preserved, not silently corrected.
